Before you ask

A few things people ask first

What does the implementation fee actually cover?

Connecting your accounting and CRM systems (QuickBooks, Dash, Salesforce, NetSuite, and more), migrating your historical A/R, configuring your offices, departments, and follow-up cadence, and training your team. It's scoped work, not a generic setup charge.

Is this a long-term contract?

Starter and Growth are billed monthly with no long-term lock-in. Enterprise agreements are typically structured annually to match onboarding scope — details are confirmed before you sign anything.

Is our data isolated from other businesses?

Yes. Every account is tenant-isolated at the database level — not just filtered in the app, enforced by the database itself. Your data is never visible to, or combined with, another company's.

What if we outgrow our tier?

You move up whenever your AR volume calls for it. There's no penalty for growing — we'd rather you upgrade than feel boxed in.
