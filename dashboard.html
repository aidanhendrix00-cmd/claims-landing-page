Autonomous Cadence Follow-Ups
Follow-ups, the Day-30 Demand Letter, and the Day-40 NOIL are drafted and sent automatically as each invoice crosses a cadence checkpoint — no manual "send" click required.
CADENCE: 1 · 3 · 5 · 7 · 10 · 15 · 25 · 30 (Demand) · 40 (NOIL)
Require Review Before Sending
When enabled, cadence checkpoints are logged as "ready to send" but held for manual approval instead of sending automatically. Off by default, per your workflow.
APPLIES TO: Cadence follow-ups, demand letters, NOILs

Payment Request Auto-Send

TRIGGER: Job Status → In A/R + Docs Complete
