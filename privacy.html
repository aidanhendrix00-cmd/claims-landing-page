Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026 — Version 1
In short: clAIms stores the information your company puts into the Service and the invoice, payment and customer records you choose to sync from connected systems such as QuickBooks Online or a connected mailbox. 1. Who we are

clAIms is an accounts-receivable collections platform operated by HNDRX Enterprise LLC, doing business as clAIms Collection ("clAIms," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains what information we collect when you use clAIms (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies alongside our Terms & Conditions.

2. Information we collect

Account information you give us. Your name, work email address, password (stored only as a salted hash), company name, company address and size, office location, and billing details. Payment card data is collected and processed by Stripe; clAIms never sees or stores full card numbers.

Operational data your company enters. Invoices, customers, points of contact, notes, uploaded documents (estimates, work authorizations, photos), follow-up settings, and the messages your team sends from the dashboard.

Data synced from connected systems. If your company's administrator connects an accounting system or data source, we import the records needed to run collections: invoices (number, dates, amounts, balances, custom fields such as a claim or purchase-order number, invoice messages and memos), payments applied to those invoices (amount, date, method, reference), and the customer contact details attached to them (name, contact person, email, phone, billing address). Section 4 describes each integration.

Activity and usage information. Actions taken in the Service (follow-ups sent, notes added, invoices updated) attributed to the team member who took them, sign-in events, and standard web logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps) used for security and troubleshooting.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: build your invoice queue, aging and collections reports, draft and send follow-ups, record payments, and keep your dashboard in sync with connected systems.
  • To draft follow-up text. Invoice details (customer, amount, age, prior correspondence) may be sent to an AI model provider to draft a follow-up email for your review. The provider does not use this content to train its models.
  • To run your account: billing, plan limits, team invitations, support requests, and service notices such as a weekly digest.
  • To keep the Service secure and reliable: detecting abuse, troubleshooting errors, and enforcing our Terms.
  • To comply with law and to establish or defend legal claims.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your company's data to market to your customers.

4. Connected systems

QuickBooks Online. A company administrator may connect the company's QuickBooks Online account through Intuit's own consent screen. clAIms requests the QuickBooks accounting scope only and uses it to read invoices, payments, customers and company information. clAIms does not create, change or delete any record in QuickBooks and does not access payroll, banking or payment-processing data. Intuit notifies clAIms when an invoice, payment or customer changes so the dashboard stays current. The access and refresh tokens Intuit issues are stored encrypted, are never shown to users, and are used only to read the data described here. Disconnecting QuickBooks (from the Integrations tab, or from within QuickBooks) revokes clAIms' access with Intuit and stops all further synchronization. Your use of QuickBooks Online remains subject to Intuit's privacy statement.

Connected email (Google or Microsoft). A user may authorize clAIms to send follow-up emails from their own mailbox. clAIms requests send-only permission, never reads, searches or stores the contents of the mailbox, and stores the sign-in tokens encrypted. Details are in Section 8A of the Terms.

Other accounting or CRM systems. Where the clAIms team wires an integration for your company, the same rules apply: only invoice, payment and customer records needed for collections are imported, and credentials are stored encrypted.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with the providers that help us run the Service, each under contract to use it solely for that purpose:

  • Cloudflare — application hosting and network security.
  • Supabase — database and file storage (United States).
  • Stripe — subscription billing and card processing.
  • Resend — delivery of transactional email sent on clAIms' behalf.
  • Anthropic — drafting follow-up text from invoice details, when you use AI drafting.
  • Intuit, Google, Microsoft — only when your company connects those systems, and only the data exchange described in Section 4.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights or safety of clAIms or others, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply to the information transferred. Other users in your company see the data your company's roles allow them to see; administrators control who is on the team.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your company's data stays in its workspace for as long as the company's clAIms account is active. Records imported from a connected system remain after that system is disconnected until your company deletes them. When a company closes its account, we delete the workspace and its data within 30 days, except for billing records we must keep for tax and accounting purposes and backups that expire on a rolling schedule. You may request deletion of specific records, or of your whole workspace, by writing to us at the address in Section 12.

7. Security

All traffic to and from the Service is encrypted in transit (TLS). Data is encrypted at rest by our hosting providers. Credentials for connected systems and passwords receive additional protection: passwords are salted and hashed, and integration tokens are encrypted with a per-record key that is itself encrypted with a master key held only in our hosting environment's secret store. Access to production systems is limited to the people who operate the Service. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will notify your company's administrator without undue delay.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction. Most account and company information can be viewed and edited in the Service. For anything else, contact us.
  • Export. Your company's administrator can request an export of the company's records.
  • Deletion. You may ask us to delete your personal information or your company's workspace, subject to Section 6.
  • Connected systems. An administrator can disconnect QuickBooks or any integration at any time; a user can disconnect their mailbox at any time.
  • Email. Service notices are part of the Service; marketing email, if we ever send any, will include an unsubscribe link.

Residents of California and other states with privacy laws may have additional rights, including the right to know what personal information we hold and to request its deletion. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising. To exercise any right, contact us; we will verify the request and respond within the time the applicable law requires.

9. Cookies

clAIms uses a single session cookie to keep you signed in. It is marked HttpOnly and Secure, expires after seven days, and contains no personal information. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

10. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed, and we will notify company administrators of material changes by email or in the Service before they take effect.
